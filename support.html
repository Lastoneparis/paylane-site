Frequently asked questions

Are the tax and contribution rates official?

No. Each country template is pre-filled with typical rates as a convenient starting point. Every rate and line is fully editable, and PayLane is not accounting, tax, or legal advice — always verify against your local regulations.

Which countries are supported?

24 templates, including France, Switzerland, Germany, Belgium, Netherlands, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Luxembourg, the UK, Ireland, Poland, the US, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Morocco, South Africa, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, India, Japan and Australia. You can edit any template to match another country.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. PayLane has no account and no cloud — see our Privacy Policy.

How do I export a payslip?

Open a payslip and tap the share button to generate a print-ready A4 PDF, then share it by email, message or AirDrop.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes — PayLane runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
